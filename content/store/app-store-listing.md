## English (optional second localization)

### Promotional text

### Description

```
Majlis revives the majalis al-sama': the gatherings where books of hadith were read aloud and studied together. Choose a hadith and listen to it, then to its manners, benefits and overall meaning: at home, on the way, or in your car.

Majlis is in Arabic: the hadith text, the narration and the interface.

THE BOOKS
• The Forty Hadith of al-Nawawi: all 42 hadiths, built into the app.
• Riyad al-Salihin: all 1,896 hadiths. The first 50 are built in; the rest are a one-time download (about 1 GB) for offline listening.

EVERY HADITH, A COMPLETE LESSON
• The hadith text, fully vowelled, read aloud at an unhurried pace.
• The manners (adab) it teaches.
• Its benefits and lessons (fawa'id).
• Its overall meaning in one paragraph.
• Related Qur'anic verses where there are any, and the commentaries the explanation draws on, such as Ibn Rajab's Jami' al-'Ulum wal-Hikam, Ibn Daqiq al-'Id's commentary on the Forty, and Ibn 'Uthaymin's commentary on Riyad al-Salihin.

LISTEN YOUR WAY
• The hadith plays, then its manners, benefits and meaning, and the session moves on to the next hadith by itself.
• Choose in Settings which parts of the commentary are read.
• The screen follows the part being read, so you see what you hear.
• Playback continues in the background and with the phone locked, with controls on the lock screen.

IN THE CAR WITH CARPLAY
• Browse the hadiths on the car's screen and pick any one.
• Now Playing shows the hadith and the part being read, with artwork carrying the hadith's text.
• Control playback from the touchscreen or the steering-wheel buttons: play, pause, and move between the parts of a hadith.
• Works in the car without opening the app on the phone; just open it once after installing or updating.

YOUR JOURNEY
• Mark each hadith you finish and follow your progress through each book.
• "Continue your last majlis" takes you back to where you stopped.
• Your progress and settings stay on your device.

A WAQF FOR THE SAKE OF ALLAH
Majlis is open source under the Waqf General Public License: no ads, no subscriptions, no account, no tracking.
```
